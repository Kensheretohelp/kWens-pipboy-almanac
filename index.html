Growing Notes

Reminder: grow bags dry out faster than beds. Water before fertilizing if soil is dry.

Fertilizer shorthand: 10-10-10 = balanced; 5-10-10 = lower nitrogen for flowers/fruit; herbs need lighter feeding.

Garlic: plant in fall for next summer harvest. It lives on a different rhythm than your summer crops.
