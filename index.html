Growing Notes

Grow bags: they dry faster than beds, especially during heat. Check daily in July/August and water before fertilizing if soil is dry.

Fruit set: this means the flower has dropped and a tiny tomato or pepper is visibly forming. That is when weekly fruiting fertilizer begins.

Tomato recovery mode: if cold-damaged leaves look ugly but new growth is green, keep the plant, remove dead leaves only, and resume feeding once active growth returns.

Heat rule: during 28°C+ days, check containers more often. At 31°C+, consider afternoon shade for basil, peppers, and stressed transplants.
